Coinbase.com Listing Guideline

How to List your Coins, Tokens, Crypto Assets on Coinbase — the Leading Crypto Exchange in the USA.

Coinbase's goal is to provide a neutral marketplace where customers can engage with every safe and compliant crypto asset. Listing an asset on Coinbase is completely free of all fees and prerequisite costs — it always has been. (Source: Coinbase Blog, May 31 2022 — Listing assets on Coinbase is free, and always has been)

How the Coinbase asset listing process works

Our philosophy is to list every safe and compliant asset — we're not here to pick winners or losers. Coinbase applies a rigorous asset evaluation process to ensure assets meet eligibility requirements:

  • Apply. Asset issuers start by creating an account in Coinbase Asset Hub and submitting an application with all relevant information about their asset.
  • Evaluate. Coinbase's Digital Asset Listing Group (DALG) evaluates the information using a standard listing evaluation framework covering legal, compliance, and technical security reviews.
  • List. Once approved by DALG and integrated with the platform, the asset can be listed on the exchange. Being listed does not mean Coinbase endorses the asset.
  • Monitor. Coinbase constantly monitors all assets and may de-list assets if they stop meeting requirements.

Coinbase offers over 212 assets for custody and 172 assets for trading. Coinbase Earn allows asset issuers to launch educational campaigns (Inquire → Design → Launch → Earn) — whether or not an Earn campaign is run has no impact on listing approval.

Binance.com Listing Guideline

How to Get Your Coin Listed on Binance.com (2021-06-18 — Binance FAQ)

Binance aims to provide more access to crypto and makes best efforts to list high-quality tokens. Token listing on Binance goes through rigorous due diligence.

How to apply

  • Fill in the online application forms for direct listing on Binance.com and Launchpad / Launchpool at binance.com/en/my/coin-apply.
  • Direct listing and Launchpool are open to projects that either have tokens circulating or have not issued a coin yet; Launchpad is limited to earlier-stage projects that have not held a token generation event.
  • The application must be filled out by the project founder or CEO, including all information you wish to communicate.

What improves your chances

  • Update your project progress to the community and Binance regularly (weekly or monthly) — required even after listing.
  • Incorporate BNB into your ecosystem and/or raise BNB during your fundraising phase.
  • Support Binance in your community.

Important notes

  • Do not ask for status updates from the CEO or a contact person; contacting the review team directly is not allowed.
  • Do not "shill" coins to the CEO on social media; provide regular status updates through the listing application form.
