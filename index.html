Chinese Summary（中文摘要）

本書由香港區塊鏈學會 (HKBA) 張俊勇 (Thomas Cheung) 撰寫, 核心主題圍繞區塊鏈即信任機器展開。書中剖析傳統商業依賴中心化中介建立信任的固有痛點, 講解區塊鏈如何透過共識機制、不可竄改賬本、密碼學, 建立一套 "去中介化" 的新型信任架構。

書中結合香港 Web3、數字資產、金融監管、商業落地場景, 拆解區塊鏈信任的底層邏輯; 同時探討技術信任與人文信任的邊界——區塊鏈可以保障數據可信, 但長遠商業合作, 依然離不開人的價值觀、誠信與共識。

適合 Web3 從業者、金融從業者、創業者、監管研究者閱讀, 幫助讀者理解 "代碼層面的信任" 如何和商業、社會治理結合。

English Summary

Authored by Thomas Cheung, published under the Hong Kong Blockchain Association (HKBA), In Blockchain We Trust frames blockchain fundamentally as a trust machine.

The book examines the long-standing weaknesses of trust built on centralized intermediaries in traditional commerce. It explains how blockchain creates a new trust infrastructure via cryptographic proof, immutable ledgers and distributed consensus, removing the need for single trusted middlemen.

Drawing from Hong Kong's Web3 ecosystem, digital-asset regulation and real business use cases, it unpacks the mechanics of technical trust. It also delivers a balanced view: blockchain secures data integrity, yet sustainable partnerships still rely on human integrity, shared values and moral consensus.

It is written for Web3 builders, finance practitioners, entrepreneurs and regulatory researchers to understand how code-based trust integrates with commerce and social governance.

About HKBA

協會簡介

香港區塊鏈協會 HKBA.club 是一個開放、自主、平等的去中心化組織 (Decentralized Organization DAO)。通過學習、交流及推廣區塊鏈技術及應用, HKBA 致力成為鏈接大中華及 Web3 元宇宙世界的銜接平臺, 並將香港打造成新時代的 AI Blockchain 金融科技中心。

Our Story

協會沿革與使命

Founded 2017

由行業先驅創立

HKBA was formed in 2017 by our Co-Founders Tony Tong 唐儀 (TonyTong.cc), Thomas Cheung 張俊勇 (Hong Kong Blockchain Society HKBCS 香港區塊鏈學會), Prof. Wei-Tek Tsai 蔡維德 (Beihang University Blockchain Lab) and Jay Liang (GSR Matrix Fund). Since then, HKBA has grown to become the leading industry association for the Blockchain and Web3 community in Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area.

Education

大學合作 · 元宇宙教育學院

HKBA has partnered with Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), HKUST, Hong Kong Metropolitan University (HKMU), CityU, FuYao University, 1College.org (HKBA 元宇宙教育學院 — 開放的線上教育知識共用平臺), Hong Kong Financial Services Institute (香港金融管理學院 HKFSI) and International Technical University (ITU, Silicon Valley) to provide Blockchain, AI and Web3 Education.
