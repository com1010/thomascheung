2024 年 4 月 17 日

HKBA x MEAACBA 歷史性 MOU 簽署

Hong Kong Blockchain Association (HKBA.club) 與 Middle East Africa and Asia Crypto and Blockchain Association (MEAACBA.org) 簽署歷史性諒解備忘錄, 促進亞洲、中東與非洲的區塊鏈與加密貨幣技術發展。合作包括研發、教育培訓、會議研討、監管倡導及公眾意識提升等系列聯合活動。

"The partnership with MEAACBA underpins our unwavering commitment to promoting blockchain technology and digital currencies to create a more transparent, secure, and inclusive financial system." — Tony Tong, Co-Chairman of HKBA

"This MOU represents a significant milestone in the advancement of blockchain and cryptocurrency technologies across Middle East, Africa, and Asia." — Jehanzeb Awan, Chairman of MEAACBA
